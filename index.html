About the Project

This project explores a student dataset to understand patterns involving sleep duration, screen usage, study habits, attendance and CGPA.

Data analysis helps us investigate relationships between lifestyle choices and academic performance. A relationship does not necessarily mean that one factor causes another.

Key Research Questions

  • How is weekday sleep duration related to academic performance?
  • What patterns exist between daily screen time and CGPA?
  • How do study hours and class attendance relate to grades?
  • Why are sleep, concentration and healthy routines important for students?
